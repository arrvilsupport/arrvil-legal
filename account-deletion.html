Arrvil Music Player · Account support

Delete your AMP account

You can request deletion of an Arrvil Music Player (AMP) account either from the Android app or from this page. You do not need to reinstall AMP to request deletion.

Option 1 — Delete inside AMP

  1. Open Arrvil Music Player and sign in to the Google account linked to the AMP account.
  2. Open Account and choose Delete AMP Account.
  3. Review the confirmation and approve deletion. For security, Google may ask you to sign in again.
  4. AMP will attempt to delete the Firebase Authentication account and then clear locally saved account-profile fields. Wait for a confirmed result.

Sign Out alone does not delete your AMP account.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

Email arrvil.support@gmail.com from the email address associated with your AMP Google sign-in, if possible. Use the subject AMP Account Deletion Request. Tell us that you want your Arrvil Music Player account and related account data deleted.

Email account deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, manually send an email to arrvil.support@gmail.com. You may request deletion even if AMP has already been uninstalled.

We may contact you to verify that you own the account before completing the deletion. We will explain if any information must be retained for legal or security reasons. We will confirm the outcome by email. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, government IDs or music files.

What is deleted?

  • AMP Firebase Authentication account: deleted after the request is verified and successfully processed.
  • Local sign-in information: in-app account deletion clears the saved profile name, email, profile-photo URL and signed-in flag on that device.
  • Other backend records: if any account-associated records are identified, they must be assessed and removed or their lawful retention explained. The current app audit did not identify a separate AMP-operated profile database.

What remains?

  • Your Google account: not deleted.
  • Personal music files: not deleted by deleting the AMP account.
  • Local playlists, favorites, library cache and preferences: remain on the device after in-app account deletion unless you separately clear the app's data. ARRVIL cannot remotely erase files on a device it does not control.
  • Support correspondence or records required by law: may be kept as needed to process requests or meet legitimate obligations.

To remove AMP's remaining on-device app data, you can use Android's app storage controls. This does not remove personal music files stored elsewhere on the device.

Questions?

For help with deletion, contact arrvil.support@gmail.com. For information about other data flows and providers, read the Privacy Policy.
