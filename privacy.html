Privacy Policy

Application: Arrvil Music Player (AMP) · Android package: com.arrvil.musicplayer
Developer: ARRVIL · Effective date: 12 November 2026

Arrvil Music Player is an offline Android music player for audio stored on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses and stores, and how sharing and support work.

Scope

This policy covers the offline ARRVIL 1.0.x release and ARRVIL 2.0 once available. AMP scans and plays audio stored on your device and can open audio you select in another Android app. AMP has no sign-in or user accounts. These releases have no advertising, analytics, automatic crash-report submission, online lyrics, online artwork search, cloud synchronization or embedded tag editing.

The in-app update section below applies only to eligible Google Play installations of ARRVIL 2.0 once it is released.

Music access and Android permissions

With your permission, AMP uses Android's media library (MediaStore) to find local songs, albums, artists and folders and to play your music. On Android 13 and later, AMP requests READ_MEDIA_AUDIO. On supported older Android versions, it uses READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, limited to Android 12L and earlier. You can deny or revoke audio access in Android Settings; AMP's ability to scan or play the affected music will then be limited. Android controls the permission prompts and access to each file.

AMP reads local audio information such as titles, artists, albums, file names and paths or content URIs, media identifiers, durations, sizes and file timestamps. It reads embedded artwork for the library, player and artwork-based colors. AMP processes this audio and information on your device and does not automatically send your library, listening information or artwork to ARRVIL or an online music service.

AMP uses Android foreground media-playback services, media notifications and wake locks to continue playback and provide notification and lock-screen controls. Song titles, artists and artwork may appear on those Android surfaces and connected media controls. Their visibility also depends on your device's notification and lock-screen settings. Native playback components may check Android's network status as part of playback.

Information stored on your device

AMP stores preferences, cached library details and temporary copies in app-private storage protected by Android's app access controls. These records include the following:

  • Custom playlists, favorite-song selections, library sorting and cached song, album and artist information.
  • Playback preferences and restoration information, including the last selected song and playback position, shuffle and repeat, headphone behavior, theme and animation choices.
  • Equalizer, audio effects, and appearance and guidance preferences, including additional choices in 2.0.
  • Optional song-title and artist display overrides that you enter in AMP.

Song-information overrides change labels inside AMP only. They do not rewrite the audio, its embedded tags or embedded cover image. AMP retains the original metadata and uses local file identity information to associate overrides with the right song. An override may be retired when a successful library scan determines that the original file was removed or replaced. You can use Restore Original Information in that song's editor to remove its override. Overrides do not synchronize to other apps or devices through an ARRVIL service.

Artwork cache and retention

AMP caches local artwork for display and creates temporary JPEG thumbnails for Android's media notification. Background Sync refreshes artwork from local embedded artwork.

Notification thumbnail cleanup runs after every 16 new thumbnails and targets 64 retained files. It protects files currently in use, still remembered by the app or being written. The count can exceed the target between cleanup passes or while files are protected. Older thumbnails can remain until a later pass or Android cache clearing. Android may reclaim other cached artwork, and ending the app process clears in-memory artwork. Thumbnail cleanup leaves your original music and embedded artwork intact.

Temporary audio copies

When you use Open With, AMP makes a full temporary copy in its private cache to read the track details and play it locally. Cleanup can run on playback handoffs and skips the copy reported as current. Older copies can remain until another cleanup pass or Android cache clearing. If the file comes from another provider, that provider handles access to the original under its own practices.

When you share a song, AMP can stage a temporary copy for Android to hand to the app you choose. The sharing component attempts to clear its staging cache at the start of a subsequent share. A copy can remain until another share or Android cache clearing. The receiving app may keep its own copy.

Backup and device transfer

AMP disables automatic Android cloud backup of its app-private data. Its backup rules exclude app-private storage and preferences on supported older Android backup systems. Its Android 12-and-later rules also exclude that data from cloud backup and supported device-transfer mechanisms. These settings do not erase existing playlists, favorites or overrides.

Device manufacturers and independent migration, recovery or backup tools may handle transfers differently. Backups created by older app versions may still exist. Your shared-storage music and backups you create using other apps or services are controlled separately from AMP's app-private backup rules. AMP does not operate its own cloud-backup or synchronization service.

Sharing and deleting music

When you choose Share, AMP passes the selected audio and its displayed title and artist to Android's chooser. If the file is unavailable, the share may contain only the title and artist. Android can grant the receiving app access to the shared file. That app handles any sending, uploading or further use under its own privacy practices.

When you choose to delete music, AMP asks for confirmation and uses Android's supported mechanism, including an additional system consent prompt where Android requires it. Depending on Android's available capabilities, the action moves the actual file to Android's Trash or permanently deletes it; the confirmation identifies which action applies. Trash retention and restoration are controlled by Android. Permanent deletion cannot be undone through AMP. The action also removes the song from AMP's playlists, favorites and queue and can affect other music apps. Denying or cancelling the request does not authorize deletion. Removing a song from a playlist or favorites does not itself delete the audio file.

External links and support

The Privacy Policy entry in Settings opens the public HTTPS policy in an external browser or another Android-selected handler. Contact Support opens your email app addressed to arrvil.support@gmail.com. You decide whether to send a message and what to include. AMP does not send a support message or attach your library automatically.

The policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open an external page or use an email service, that provider may process ordinary connection information or the message under its own practices. Those interactions take place outside AMP's local playback. Avoid including private music files or sensitive information in support messages unless you intend to share them.

If you contact ARRVIL support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to and investigate your request.

Support correspondence is stored in our Gmail inbox until manually deleted. We do not currently use an automatic deletion schedule for support emails.

You can contact arrvil.support@gmail.com to request deletion of your support correspondence. We will review such requests, taking into account any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Google Play in-app updates in ARRVIL 2.0

On eligible Google Play installations of ARRVIL 2.0, AMP may check for updates when the app is in the foreground. You can also choose Check for Updates in Settings. On your device, AMP saves the last check time, a reminder for the update version you dismiss before download and a flag to check for an unfinished update when you return. You choose whether to download an offered update and when to install it. Google Play handles the download and installation. Your usual Google Play update settings apply separately.

AMP calls Google's Play in-app update API. Google Play may process device metadata, the installed app version and information about installed modules or asset packs to determine update availability and size. Google says it encrypts this information, deletes it after a set retention period and does not transfer it to third parties. AMP's update requests do not include your local songs, library metadata, playlists, Favorites, album art or listening history. Google Play SDK Data safety information.

Your controls, retention and app removal

Local playlists, preferences, cached library information and overrides generally remain until changed or removed by the relevant app function, Android storage clearing, app removal, or applicable library reconciliation. Cache retention works as described above; Android can also reclaim temporary files sooner.

You can revoke audio access or clear AMP's cache or app storage through Android Settings. Clearing cache removes temporary data without intentionally clearing saved playlists or overrides; clearing app storage/data removes AMP's local saved state. Uninstalling AMP removes its app-private state under Android's normal behavior. Clearing AMP storage or uninstalling it does not instruct AMP to delete your shared-storage music. Music is deleted only through an authorized deletion operation or another app/device action. Device-transfer tools or previously created external backups can retain independent copies beyond AMP's control.

Changes and contact

ARRVIL may update this policy when the app or its data practices change and will identify the effective revision on the public policy page. For questions about AMP's privacy practices or this policy, contact arrvil.support@gmail.com.
