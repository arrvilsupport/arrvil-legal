Privacy Policy

Application: Arrvil Music Player (AMP) · Android package: com.arrvil.musicplayer
Developer: ARRVIL · Last updated: 30 October 2026

Arrvil Music Player is an offline Android music player for audio stored on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses and stores, and how sharing and support work.

Scope

This policy describes the simplified, offline Release V1 of Arrvil Music Player. AMP scans and plays audio stored on your device. It does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics services. Release V1 does not offer sign-in, online lyrics, online artwork search, cloud synchronization, or editing of audio files' embedded tags.

Older AMP versions may have offered different features. The legacy-account information below is separate from Release V1.

Music access and Android permissions

With your permission, AMP uses Android's media library (MediaStore) to find local songs, albums, artists and folders and to play your music. On Android 13 and later, AMP requests READ_MEDIA_AUDIO. On supported older Android versions, it uses READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, limited to Android 12L and earlier. You can deny or revoke audio access in Android Settings; AMP's ability to scan or play the affected music will then be limited. Android controls the permission prompts and access to each file.

AMP reads local audio information such as titles, artists, albums, file names and paths or content URIs, media identifiers, durations, sizes and file timestamps. It reads available embedded album artwork to display songs and player backgrounds. The audio and this information are processed on your device for the music-player functions; AMP does not automatically send your library, listening information, or artwork to ARRVIL or an online music service.

AMP uses Android foreground media-playback services, media notifications and wake locks to continue playback and provide notification and lock-screen controls. Song titles, artists and artwork may appear on those Android surfaces and connected media controls. Their visibility also depends on your device's notification and lock-screen settings. Native playback dependencies can check network state; that permission does not itself provide Internet access or upload your library.

Information stored on your device

AMP stores app-private preferences and library information needed for its functions. These include:

  • Custom playlists, favorite-song selections, library sorting and cached song, album and artist information.
  • Playback preferences and restoration information, including the last selected song and playback position, shuffle and repeat, headphone behavior, theme and animation choices.
  • Equalizer and audio-effect settings.
  • Optional song-title and artist display overrides that you enter in AMP.

Song-information overrides change labels inside AMP only. They do not rewrite the audio, its embedded tags or embedded cover image. AMP retains the original metadata and uses local file identity information to associate overrides with the right song. An override may be retired when a successful library scan determines that the original file was removed or replaced. You can use Restore Original Information in that song's editor to remove its override. Overrides do not synchronize to other apps or devices through an ARRVIL service.

Artwork cache and retention

AMP caches local artwork for display and creates temporary JPEG thumbnails for Android's media notification. Background Sync refreshes artwork from local embedded artwork; it does not fetch covers from the Internet or upload them.

For AMP's dedicated notification-thumbnail directory, maintenance runs after every 16 newly written thumbnails. It removes older AMP-generated thumbnails and targets 64 retained files. Files still remembered by the artwork resolver, currently used by the media session, or being written are protected. The count can temporarily exceed the target between maintenance passes or while protected files remain in use. This is an opportunistic file-count limit, not a fixed retention period or a guaranteed byte-size limit. If no more thumbnails are written, old files can remain until a later maintenance pass or Android cache clearing. Other temporary or in-memory artwork caches may be reclaimed by Android or by ending the app process. Thumbnail cleanup does not delete your music or its embedded artwork.

Backup and device transfer

Release V1 disables automatic Android cloud backup of AMP's app-private data. Its backup rules exclude app-private storage and preferences on supported older Android backup systems. Its Android 12-and-later rules also exclude that data from cloud backup and supported device-transfer mechanisms. These settings do not erase existing playlists, favorites or overrides.

Device manufacturers and independent migration, recovery or backup tools may behave differently; AMP cannot guarantee that every device-transfer tool honors these exclusions. Backups created by older app versions are not retroactively deleted by this setting. Your shared-storage music and backups you create using other apps or services are controlled separately from AMP's app-private backup rules. AMP does not operate its own cloud-backup or synchronization service.

Sharing and deleting music

When you choose Share, AMP asks Android to share the selected local audio through an app you select. Android may grant that recipient access to the shared file. Any sending, uploading or further handling is performed by the chosen app and is subject to that app's privacy practices. AMP does not automatically share your songs.

When you choose to delete music, AMP asks for confirmation and uses Android's supported mechanism, including an additional system consent prompt where Android requires it. Depending on Android's available capabilities, the action moves the actual file to Android's Trash or permanently deletes it; the confirmation identifies which action applies. Trash retention and restoration are controlled by Android. Permanent deletion cannot be undone through AMP. The action also removes the song from AMP's playlists, favorites and queue and can affect other music apps. Denying or cancelling the request does not authorize deletion. Removing a song from a playlist or favorites does not itself delete the audio file.

External links and support

The Privacy Policy entry in Settings opens the public policy in an external browser or another Android-selected handler. Contact Support opens your email app addressed to arrvil.support@gmail.com; you decide whether to send a message and what to include. AMP does not send a support message or attach your library automatically.

The policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open an external page or use an email service, that provider may process ordinary connection information or the message under its own practices. Those interactions take place outside AMP's local playback. Avoid including private music files or sensitive information in support messages unless you intend to share them.

If you contact ARRVIL, ARRVIL receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Support correspondence is used to answer and investigate your request and retained as needed to handle it, follow up and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may contact the same address to ask about removal of support correspondence. AMP has no automatic analytics or crash-report submission feature; diagnostics you choose to send become part of your support correspondence.

Your controls, retention and app removal

Local playlists, preferences, cached library information and overrides generally remain until changed or removed by the relevant app function, Android storage clearing, app removal, or applicable library reconciliation. Cache retention works as described above; Android can also reclaim temporary files sooner.

You can revoke audio access or clear AMP's cache or app storage through Android Settings. Clearing cache removes temporary data without intentionally clearing saved playlists or overrides; clearing app storage/data removes AMP's local saved state. Uninstalling AMP removes its app-private state under Android's normal behavior. Clearing AMP storage or uninstalling it does not instruct AMP to delete your shared-storage music. Music is deleted only through an authorized deletion operation or another app/device action. Device-transfer tools or previously created external backups can retain independent copies beyond AMP's control.

Legacy accounts from earlier AMP versions

Release V1 has no accounts or account-deletion screen. If you used an account in an earlier full-featured AMP version, ARRVIL's legacy account-deletion request page remains separate from Release V1. Contact arrvil.support@gmail.com for assistance with a legacy request. Installing or using Release V1 does not itself delete a legacy account. For details of earlier account-enabled AMP versions, see the archived privacy policy.

Changes and contact

ARRVIL may update this policy when the app or its data practices change and will identify the effective revision on the public policy page. For questions about AMP's privacy practices or this policy, contact arrvil.support@gmail.com.
