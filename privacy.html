Privacy Policy

Application: Arrvil Music Player (AMP) · Android package: com.arrvil.musicplayer
Developer: ARRVIL · Last updated: 26 October 2026

Arrvil Music Player ("AMP", "we", "our") is a music player for audio files stored on your Android device. This policy explains the data AMP accesses, stores and sends when you choose to use its features.

1. Music files and device access

With Android's permission, AMP reads information about audio files on your device, such as titles, artists, albums, duration and artwork, so it can display your library and play music. AMP also lets you manage local playlists and favorites, edit supported MP3 metadata, choose cover images, and request that Android delete or trash selected music files. File deletion requires a user action.

Your music files are not uploaded to an ARRVIL-operated music server as part of normal offline playback. If you choose to share an MP3, Android's share sheet sends that file to the app or recipient you select; that recipient's handling of the file is governed by its own practices.

2. Google Sign-In and account information

Google Sign-In is optional; AMP also supports guest use. If you sign in, Google and Firebase Authentication process information needed to authenticate you, which may include an account identifier, display name, email address and profile image URL. AMP stores selected account profile fields and a sign-in status locally to display the account within the app. Google and Firebase have their own privacy practices for the services they operate.

3. Information stored on your device

AMP may store local playlists, favorites, library metadata or cache, playback preferences, Equalizer settings, theme settings and imported lyric files on your device. These records are used to provide your music-player experience. This release does not implement cloud synchronization of playlists or favorites.

4. Optional online features

When you use online lyrics lookup, AMP sends relevant song information—such as title, artist, and potentially album and duration—to LRCLIB. When you search for online cover art, AMP sends title/artist search terms to MusicBrainz and may request images from Cover Art Archive. Those services, and their hosting networks, can receive technical connection data such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies and retention practices apply. Do not use these features if you do not wish to make those requests.

Viewing or importing local lyrics and using artwork from your device does not require these optional online lookups.

5. Permissions

AMP requests access to audio files to provide its core offline music library. It uses network access for optional online features and Google/Firebase sign-in. Android background media playback may use a foreground service and media notification. Gallery-based artwork selection uses an Android file or photo picker instead of broad photo-library access in the audited release.

6. Third-party services

Depending on the features you use, data or requests may be processed by Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, LRCLIB, MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive. AMP does not control these services' independent handling of data. The inspected release did not include a dedicated advertising or analytics SDK; this description should be reviewed if later versions add one.

7. Security

AMP relies on Android's application storage protections, Android permission controls and HTTPS connections used by its online clients. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Never send your Google password, keystore passwords or other secrets to support.

8. Retention and deletion

Local playlists, favorites, settings and library records remain on the device until you remove them using available app or Android controls or clear application storage. Signing out clears locally stored account profile information, but does not itself delete the Firebase Authentication account. Choosing Delete AMP Account in the app initiates deletion of the Firebase Authentication account and, after success, clears locally stored sign-in/profile fields. Your Google account and personal music files are not deleted. Local playlists, favorites, cached library data and app settings remain on the device.

Users who cannot access the app may request account deletion using our public deletion page. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. Requests and related support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to document or complete the request and comply with applicable obligations; contact us for details about a particular request. Any information retained by independent providers is subject to their own policies and applicable controls.

9. Children's privacy

AMP is a general-purpose local music player. We have not designed it specifically for children. If you believe a child's personal information has been provided through an AMP account without appropriate authorization, contact us to request review or deletion.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as AMP's functions or provider integrations change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision. Material changes should be reflected in the app and applicable store disclosures.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or account deletion support, contact ARRVIL at arrvil.support@gmail.com.
